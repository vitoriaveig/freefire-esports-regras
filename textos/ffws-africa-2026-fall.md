Overview
 This rulebook serves as the official guide created by Garena to regulate Free Fire World Series Africa
 2026 Fall Split, and sets out the rules, tournament procedures, and prohibitions that all teams and players
 are required to observe. This Rulebook is intended to foster a fair and competitive environment that
 upholds the values of sportsmanship.


Definitions

   Participants                Teams and Players who are registered, verified, and deemed eligible by the
                               tournament organisers for participation in FFWS Africa.


   Team Lineup                 6 players that represent a team and participate in the Free Fire World Series
                               tournament

   Match                       Battle Royale

                               A single competitive map of either Bermuda, Purgatory, Kalahari, Nexterra or
                               Solara being played by a maximum of 12 teams in Battle Royale mode until a
                               “BOOYAH” is achieved

   Squad                       Team size of 4 Players that will participate in the game




Purpose & Position
 To showcase the highest tier of competitive players/teams to our Free Fire fans, Free Fire has created
 the “Free Fire World Series AFRICA”, a regional tournament for Sub-Saharan Africa (SSA).

  This Rulebook establishes the general rules, terms, and conditions governing participation in FFWS
  Africa 2026 Fall Split. Owing to evolving circumstances, the tournament organisers may update, amend,
  or supplement this Rulebook from time to time. Any material change will be communicated to the Team
  Captain or Manager using the contact details provided at registration or through official tournament
  communication channels. Any deviation from, or exemption to, this Rulebook is subject to the prior
  approval of the tournament organisers.

Adjustment
 Garena reserves the right to remove or modify any rule set out in this Rulebook at its discretion,
 including in order to preserve the competitive integrity of the tournament, ensure fair play, or
 address unforeseen technical or administrative issues. Garena further reserves the right to
 adjudicate any matter not expressly addressed in this Rulebook in order to maintain fair and
 sportsmanlike competition. Any change to this Rulebook will be documented and
 communicated to all parties involved in the tournament, whether directly or indirectly.


Validity
 If any provision of this Rulebook is held to be invalid or unenforceable, in whole or in part, the
 validity and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not be affected. Any invalid
 provision shall be replaced by the lawful provision that most closely reflects the original intent
 and purpose of this Rulebook.

 information may be published or disclosed without the prior written approval of Garena. In the

 ranging from a warning to a monetary fine, disqualification, or further disciplinary action.

2. Participation

 By registering for or qualifying to FFWS Africa 2026 Fall Split, teams and players agree to abide by the rules
 stated in this Rulebook and any other regulations set forth by Garena.

   2.1.     Team Obligations
            Team must adhere to all the rules set & declared by Garena as Follows:

            2.1.1. Team ownership
                      2.1.1.1.    An organisation can only own a team if they are an entity or group legally
                                  registered and recognized under a country’s corporate law or companies act or
                                  equivalent.

                      2.1.1.2.    An entity or group is considered the team owner when registered as such during
                                  the official registration process for FFWS Africa 2026 Fall Split.

                      2.1.1.3.    Teams are permitted to transfer ownership via acquisition by another
                                  organisation prior to the Roster Lock date, subject to Garena approval.
                                  Ownership transfers after Roster Lock will not be permitted unless under
                                  exceptional circumstances approved by Garena.

                      2.1.1.4.    An entity or group may only own 1 participating in FFWS Africa 2026 Fall
                                  Split. Multiple team ownership within the same tournament is strictly
                                  prohibited

                      2.1.1.5.    Slots to a tournament will be wholly owned by the team’s organisation.

                      2.1.1.6.    In the event an organisation disbands its roster and acquires another roster:
                                          2.1.1.6.1.    Invitational slots will go to the organisation regardless of
                                                        which players are in the team’s roster.
                                          2.1.1.6.2.    Qualification slots are still subject to the team meeting the
                                                        minimum required members retained from the roster that
                                                        qualified regardless of whether the team is owned by an
                                                        organisation.

                      2.1.1.7.    Should a team not be owned by any organisation, the team must declare either
                                  their manager or captain as the owner. This individual will be awarded the same
                                  rights as if they were an organisation.

                      2.1.1.8.    Garena reserves the right to be the final arbiter and decision maker in the event
                                  of any ownership disputes across all official Free Fire tournaments.

                      2.1.1.9.    The team owner, be it an organisation or individual, must be declared as part of
                                  the signing of the tournament rule book.

     2.1.2. Tournament Commitment
             By participating in FFWS AFRICA 2026 Fall, all team members commit to their exclusive
             participation in FFWS AFRICA 2026 Fall & will not join any other tournaments for the
             duration of FFWS AFRICA 2026 Fall unless approved by Garena.

Conflicts of Interest
Participants must promptly disclose to the Tournament Operator any ownership, employment, financial,
or personal relationship, whether with another team, player, official, or third party, that could affect, or be
reasonably perceived to affect, the competitive integrity of the tournament. This is a continuing
obligation, and any such relationship that arises or comes to light during the tournament must be disclosed
as soon as practicable. Failure to disclose may be treated as an integrity violation.
     2.1.3. Team Roster
                2.1.3.1.    Teams that successfully advance from Play-ins to subsequent stages must
                            retain the full roster registered at Roster Lock. No roster changes are
                            permitted after the Roster Lock date.

                2.1.3.2.    Roster size 6 players.

                2.1.3.3.    Foreign players are not permitted. All players must meet the Sub-Saharan
                            Africa eligibility requirements defined in Section 2.2.

                2.1.3.4.    Teams should finalise their roster before 26 July 2026, late roster submissions
                            will not be entertained.

                2.1.3.5.    A team captain will need to be designated among these 6 players. If the team
                            does not have a team manager, the team captain will be responsible for
                            communicating with tournament officials & providing timely updates to their
                            team members.

                2.1.3.6.    Player in-game names must be appropriate, if the player's name is deemed
                            inappropriate they have to change to the new approved in-game name.

                2.1.3.7.    Each team must register at least five (5) players and may register up to six (6)
                            players prior to the Roster Lock date. Teams with fewer than five (5)
                            registered players will not be considered eligible to participate.

                2.1.3.8.    Teams must field a minimum of four (4) players to start a match. Teams
                            unable to meet this requirement will forfeit the match.

       3.1.1. Tiebreakers
               3.1.1.1.    If there are two or more contestants tied on points at the end of a series, the
                           tiebreaker will be decided by the number of ‘Booyah’s they have achieved.

               3.1.1.2.    If the teams are also tied to the number of ‘Booyah’s, the teams will be ranked
                           according to the total number of eliminations achieved in the series.

               3.1.1.3.    Lastly, if the teams are tied in both the number of ‘Booyah’s and the total
                           number of eliminations in the series, the teams will be ranked according to their
                           placements in the last game of the series (ranking in the final game)

       3.2.4. Match Remakes
              In the event whereby a player was to encounter a bug, a remake will only be offered when all

              ●   The bug is game-breaking and causes a player (or multiple players) to be unable to play

                      ○ Being clipped through the floor and executed

                      ○ Being killed by fall damage after unexpected behaviour from driving vehicles

                      ○ Death on landing / not in the plane, etc.

              ●   A referee is promptly notified of the bug when it occurs.
              ●   The bug occurs when no other players in the match have been killed.
              ●   The bug is not triggered with malicious intent by the player.

            Remakes concerning other circumstances or bugs that were not stated above will be offered only
            at the sole discretion of the Garena officials and referees.

4.2.     Game of Record
         A game of record ("GOR") refers to a Game where all Players have loaded, and which has
         progressed to a point of meaningful interaction between opposing Teams. Once a Game attains
         GOR status, the period in which incidental restarts may be permitted ends and a Game will be
         considered as “Official” from that point onward. After the establishment of GOR, Game restarts
         will be allowed only under limited conditions.

         Examples of conditions which establish GOR: Plane enters the zone whereby players are
         allowed to drop.

         If a Player intentionally disconnects without notifying the official referee, the referee is not required
         to enforce a stoppage. During any pause or stoppage, players must remain available in the official
         communication channel or match lobby unless authorised by an official referee.
