Overview
 This rulebook serves as the official guide created by Garena to regulate Free Fire World Series Africa
 2026 Spring, outlining the rules, tournament proceedings, and prohibitions that teams and players must
 adhere to. The rulebook is designed to reinforce and foster a competitive gaming environment while
 upholding the values of sportsmanship.


Definitions

   Participants              Teams and Players who are registered, verified, and deemed eligible by the
                             tournament organisers for participation in FFWS Africa.

   Team Lineup               6 players that represent a team and participate in the Free Fire World Series
                             tournament

   Match                     Battle Royale

                             A single competitive map of either Bermuda, Purgatory, Kalahari, Nexterra or
                             Solara being played by a maximum of 12 teams in Battle Royale mode until a
                             “BOOYAH” is achieved

   Squad                     Team size of 4 Players that will participate in the game




Purpose & Position
 To showcase the highest tier of competitive players/teams to our Free Fire fans, Free Fire has created
 the “Free Fire World Series AFRICA”, a regional tournament for Sub-Saharan Africa (SSA).

  FFWS Africa 2026 Spring Split Rulebook establishes the general rules, terms, and conditions to
  participate in the tournament. Due to evolving circumstances and environment, tournament organisers
  may update, amend or supplement the rulebook from time to time. Any material changes to the
  rulebook will be provided to the Team Captain or Manager using the contact method listed in their
  registration. Deviations or exemptions from the rulebook are subject to the tournament organiser's
  approval.

Adjustment
 Garena has the right to remove or modify the rules outlined in this rulebook for any reason.
 Garena also reserves the right to make judgments in cases not specifically supported or
 detailed in this rulebook to maintain fair, competitive, and sportsmanlike gameplay in FFWS
 Africa 2026 Spring Split. Any changes to this rulebook will be documented and
 communicated to all relevant parties involved in FFWS Africa 2026 Spring Split, both
 directly and indirectly through official tournament communication channels.


Validity
 If any provision in this rulebook is deemed invalid or unenforceable, either partially or
 entirely, it will not affect the validity of the remaining rules in this rulebook. In place of such
 invalid provisions, the nearest approximation will be considered the applicable decision in
 accordance with the meaning and purpose of this rulebook.



Confidentiality
 The content of opinions, discussion outcomes, disciplinary deliberations, and other
 correspondences between participants and tournament officials is highly confidential.
 Publication or disclosure of such information is only permissible with the prior approval of
 Garena. In the event of a breach, Garena reserves the right to impose sanctions, ranging from
 warnings to fines, disqualification, or further disciplinary action against the responsible party.

2.Participation

 By registering for or qualifying to FFWS Africa 2026 Spring Split, teams and players agree to abide by the
 rules stated in this Rulebook and any other regulations set forth by Garena.

   2.1.   Team Obligations
           Team must adhere to all the rules set & declared by Garena as Follows:

           2.1.1.Team ownership
                      2.1.1.1.   An organisation can only own a team if they are an entity or group legally
                                  registered and recognized under a country’s corporate law or companies act or
                                  equivalent.

                      2.1.1.2.   An entity or group is considered the team owner when registered as such
                                  during the official registration process for FFWS Africa 2026 Spring Split.

                      2.1.1.3.   Teams are permitted to transfer ownership via acquisition by another
                                  organisation prior to the Roster Lock date, subject to Garena approval.
                                  Ownership transfers after Roster Lock will not be permitted unless under
                                  exceptional circumstances approved by Garena.

                      2.1.1.4.   An entity or group may only own 1 participating in FFWS Africa 2026 Spring
                                  Split. Multiple team ownership within the same tournament is strictly
                                  prohibited

                      2.1.1.5.   Slots to a tournament will be wholly owned by the team’s organisation.

                      2.1.1.6.   In the event an organisation disbands its roster and acquires another roster:
                                          2.1.1.6.1. Invitational slots will go to the organisation regardless of
                                                        which players are in the team’s roster.
                                          2.1.1.6.2. Qualification slots are still subject to the team meeting
                                                        the minimum required members retained from the roster
                                                        that qualified regardless of whether the team is owned by
                                                        an organisation.

                      2.1.1.7.   Should a team not be owned by any organisation, the team must declare either
                                  their manager or captain as the owner. This individual will be awarded the
                                  same rights as if they were an organisation.

                      2.1.1.8.   Garena reserves the right to be the final arbiter and decision maker in the
                                  event of any ownership disputes across all official Free Fire tournaments.

3.Game Format
 All participants of Free Fire tournaments are required to abide by the following tournament rules. Failure to
 adhere to the rules can result in warnings, fines, disqualification, or suspension from any future Free Fire
 Tournaments, as deemed appropriate by Garena and/or its officials. (refer to Disciplinary Action).

 Players will be ranked on points assigned for their placements and elimination counts at the end of each round.
 There will be a total of 6 maps being played each day. The final rankings will be determined by the total points

              For games 7 and 8, maps will be randomly selected again from the map pool. Map selections will

                            their placements in the last game of the series (ranking in the final game)

3.2.3.Costume/Skin
     Players are allowed to customise their characters' outfits with the costumes provided by
     Tournament Organizers.

     While inside a match, all opponents’ costumes will be standardised. This prevents players
     from identifying each other through their costumes.

     The broadcast content from the spectator’s client will still showcase all players in their
     original costume of choice.

     The following are prohibited:

     ● All Look Changer Emotes
     ● The Attack on Titan collaboration Titan-form skins - “Attack Titan” and “Armoured
        Titan”
     ● The Poring pet and any associated skins

3.2.4.Match Remakes
     In the event whereby a player was to encounter a bug, a remake will only be offered when all
     of the following conditions are met:

     ● The bug is game-breaking and causes a player (or multiple players) to be unable to play
        the game in a usual manner. 
        A non-exhaustive list of game-breaking bugs includes:
            ○ Being clipped through the floor and executed
            ○ Being stuck on objects (windows, launchers, etc.)
            ○ Being killed by fall damage after unexpected behaviour from driving vehicles
            ○ In-game Mic is not functioning at the start of the game
            ○ Death on landing / not in the plane, etc.
     ● The bug occurred within 5 minutes into the match.
     ● A referee is promptly notified of the bug when it occurs.
     ● The bug occurs when no other players in the match have been killed.
     ● The bug is not triggered with malicious intent by the player.

   Remakes concerning other circumstances or bugs that were not stated above will be offered
   only at the sole discretion of the Garena officials and referees.

  4.1.      Definition of Terms

        4.1.1.Unintentional Disconnection
              A Player losing connection to the Game due to problems or issues with the game client, cell
              phone, network or other devices.


        4.1.2.Intentional Disconnection
              A Player losing connection to the Game due to Player's actions (i.e. quitting the Game). Any
              actions of a Player which lead to a disconnection shall be deemed intentional, regardless of
              the actual intent of the Player.


        4.1.3.Server Crash
              All Players losing connection to a Game due to an issue with a game server, Match Server, or
              venue internet instability.


        4.1.4.Bug
              Bugs could lead to incorrect or abnormal results of the game, or even cause faults, flaws,
              malfunction or defects of game data or hardware equipment.

4.2.     Game of Record
          A game of record ("GOR") refers to a Game where all Players have loaded, and which has
          progressed to a point of meaningful interaction between opposing Teams. Once a Game attains
          GOR status, the period in which incidental restarts may be permitted ends and a Game will be
          considered as “Official” from that point onward. After the establishment of GOR, Game restarts
          will be allowed only under limited conditions.

          Examples of conditions which establish GOR: Plane enters the zone whereby players are
          allowed to drop.

4.3.     Stoppage of Play
          If a Player intentionally disconnects without notifying the official referee, the referee is not
          required to enforce a stoppage. During any pause or stoppage, players may not leave the Match
          Area unless authorised by an official referee.
